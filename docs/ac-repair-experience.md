# Replay-verified AC repair experience (Phase 7 initial slice)

Existing experience.sqlite3 remains the local store. The separate ac_repair_experience table holds at most 1000 candidate records indexed by repair study ID and candidate index. Explicitly index a committed AC evidence-repair study with --remember-ac-repair ID or /remember-ac-repair ID. Both successful and unsuccessful candidates are retained, including inconclusive observations. Ingestion independently replays the original AC report, diagnostic experiments and all repair evidence. It refuses unfinished or altered reports; no new numerical solver calls. Repeating an unchanged ingestion is idempotent. Legacy native repair experience is unchanged.

Records contain report fingerprints, study references, coarse bus/line/regulating-unit/storage/snapshot counts, diagnostic rule, base/outage class, solver version, intervention type and direction, coverage, outcome and failed rule IDs. They do not index setpoint magnitudes, individual device IDs, demand, free text or benchmark labels. A candidate is labeled verified_original_scope only if all required snapshots passed with matching versions. Comparable observed violations are rejected_observed_violation; missing/mismatched evidence is inconclusive. A known violation can coexist with unavailable snapshots; counts retain this distinction.

Use --search-ac-experience ID or /search-ac-experience ID with a committed AC cross-check study. Agent tool search_ac_repair_experience is read-only. The query is derived from replayed diagnosis, not arbitrary model-supplied symptoms. Exact rule match, matching snapshot kind and a coarse count-ratio score rank historical records. This is a retrieval heuristic, not similarity of electrical impedances, success probability, statistical evidence or a trained model. Search considers at most 1000 indexed candidates and replays at most 20 historical studies, returns up to 10 records. Reports disclose truncated scans, skipped studies and same-source matches. Indexed payloads must equal re-derived records; altered or unavailable evidence is not returned. The index and local artifacts are not cryptographically authenticated.

All retrieved directions require explicit fresh authorization and independent verification; no automatic import of old numerical settings, experiment execution or repair. The memory is local, not shared training or fine-tuning. No measured improvement in experiment efficiency or real-grid generalization yet. Subsequent work: bounded adaptive experiment selection, comparable holdout tests and richer physical similarity descriptors.
