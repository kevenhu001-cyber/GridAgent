# Polynomial NLP and local sensitivity

The mathematical program router now accepts family polynomial_nlp through the same --math-program and --math-resume entry points. Existing linear_program input and persisted reservation shape remain compatible. NLP supports 1–32 bounded continuous nonfixed variables, up to 64 polynomial constraints, 128 terms per expression and total monomial degree up to four. Variables carry descriptive units and an explicit complete initial point. Expressions are lists of coefficient/powers records, not executable strings. Integer nonlinear problems, arbitrary functions, implicit units and general MINLP are rejected.

The router chooses SciPy SLSQP with analytically compiled polynomial derivatives. It charges objective, objective-gradient, constraint and constraint-gradient callbacks against max_evaluations, with max_iterations and a cooperative monotonic deadline. Solver-internal work, final candidate evaluation, independent verification and sensitivity calculations are outside the callback count. This is not a hard process time/memory cap. The best independently feasible objective point observed is returned; local convergence reported by SLSQP is distinct from the candidate's independent feasibility. No local or global optimality certificate is claimed for this general nonconvex family.

The independent verifier directly evaluates original monomials, checking bounds, constraint residuals and objective agreement. Sensitivity reports analytical partial derivatives at the returned point and compares with bounded finite differences (central or secant near a bound). Agreement is a diagnostic, not a feasibility gate. The range-scaled magnitude ranking describes local objective sensitivity and can be zero at an optimum; it is not total system influence, a change in the optimum after reoptimization, or uncertainty quantification. Inputs should be scaled for floating-point conditioning. Units are labels; coefficients must be supplied consistently.

Example objective x^2 + y^2 with x+y=3, x,y in [0,3], initial (1,2), has analytic optimum (1.5,1.5), objective 4.5. The tests include this case, a nonlinear inequality/maximization, a mixed polynomial gradient and boundary derivative, invalid models, independent verification, budget and resume. CI must validate the implementation.
